The Goal
The goal of this game is to get closer to 21 then the dealer without going over.
Card Values
Each card is worth their face value except: Jack, Queen, and King which are worth 10 and Ace is worth either 1 or 11 (whichever benefits you the most).
The Table
You will be seated at the bottom center of the table. There will be two bots: one is to the left of you and one is to the right of you. The dealer will be sitting at the center top.
How a Round Works (Basics)
First, you must place your bets (remember that you need to bet a minimum of 10 and a maximum of 75). Then you will have multiple options. The most basic of the options is to stand (keep your total card values) or hit (get an extra card and add their card value to it).
One time buttons
Other than hitting and standing, you will have an option to surrender (give up and lose half your bet), double down (double bet and hit once), and split(creating two seperate bets if cards have the same face value) These buttons will only be available once you get your cards.
Payouts
A win pays a 1:1, while blackjack (ace and a 10 card value) pays 3:2. A tie will lead to a push or a refund of your bet while a lose will result in you losing your bet.
