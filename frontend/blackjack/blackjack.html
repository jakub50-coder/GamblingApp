The Goal
The goal of this game is to get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over or that will mean a bust and lose your bet
Card Values
Number of cards are worth their face value. Jack, Queen, and King are worth 10 and Ace is worth either 1 or 11 (whichever benefits you the most)
The Table
You sit at the botton center. Two bots sit in the left and right of you and the dealer is on the top. The dealer's second card is hidden until you stand
How a Round Works
Place your bet then press Deal. Everyone gets two cards. Choose Hit to take another carrd or Stand to hold. Then the dealers plays
Payouts
Win pays !:! while blackjack (ace and a 10 card value) pays 3:2. A tie is a push and your bet is returned. You have 15 seconds per turn.
