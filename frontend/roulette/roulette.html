Placing Bets
Select a chip value then click any spot on the table to place a bet. You can place multiple bets in one round. Click Clear to remove all bets.
Bet Types
Outside bets (Red/Black, Odd/Even, High/Low) pay 1:1. Dozens and Columns pay 2:1. A single number pays 35:1. Zero pays on Straight Up only
