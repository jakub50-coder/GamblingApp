Welcome to Baccarat!
Baccarat is one of the simplest casino games. Two hands are dealt - Player and Banker. Bet on which one will score closer to 9 or bet on a Tie!
Card Values
Ace = 1, Cards 2-9 = face value. 10, J, Q, K = 0. Add the cards and drop the ten digits. For example 7 + 8 = 15 becomes 5. Highest score of 9 wins!
Payouts
Player Wins pays 1:1 Banker wins pays 0.95:1 (5% commission). Tie pays 8:1. Player or Banker Pair pays 11:1 A Natural (8 or 9) ends the round early!
